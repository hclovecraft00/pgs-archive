Postgrowth Singapore is a community exploring post-growth ideas and principles in Singapore: alternatives to an economy built on limitless growth.

This is our resource archive. It gathers the papers, books, articles, talks and communities that members and researchers have shared with us, sorted by theme rather than by date, so you can see what we care about at a glance.

Reading the map

Each dot is one resource, and its shape shows what kind. The deeper the green, the more we’ve gathered on that theme. A dot sitting between two patches belongs to both. Tap a theme to browse what’s in it, and a dot to read about it.

Sharing something

The archive grows as people share. To suggest a paper, article or talk, message one of our organisers on Telegram, or email postgrowthsg@gmail.com.
